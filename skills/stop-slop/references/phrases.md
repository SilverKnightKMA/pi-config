# Phrase inventories (English + Vietnamese)

Presence of one item is weak evidence; density is the signal (SKILL.md rule 8). Lists are tiered: tier 1 rides in SKILL.md; everything else lives here.

## Tier 1 — AI vocabulary (frequency-validated: Kobak et al. 14.2M abstracts)

delve, tapestry, underscore, showcase, pivotal, crucial, intricate, meticulous, vibrant, testament, boasts, robust, seamless, leverage, utilize, landscape (abstract), navigate (challenges), unpack (analysis), game-changer, deep dive

## Throat-clearing openers — delete, state the content

"Here's the thing:", "Here's what/why [X]", "The uncomfortable truth is", "It turns out", "The real [X] is", "Let me be clear", "The truth is,", "I'll say it again:", "I'm going to be honest", "Can we talk about", "Here's the problem though"

## Emphasis crutches

"Full stop.", "Let that sink in.", "This matters because", "Make no mistake", "Here's why that matters"

## Meta-commentary and performative emphasis

"Hint:", "Plot twist:", "Spoiler:", "You already know this, but", "But that's another post", "[X] is a feature, not a bug", "The rest of this essay explains…", "Let me walk you through…", "In this section, we'll…", "I want to explore…", "I promise"

## Filler and hedges (cut only when they change nothing — hedging itself is human)

really, just, literally, genuinely, honestly, simply, actually · "At its core", "In today's [X]", "It's worth noting", "At the end of the day", "When it comes to", "In a world where", "The reality is"

## Fake authority (always flag; deterministic: no citation = fail)

"studies show", "experts agree", "research suggests", "it is widely known", "many believe" — each needs a named study, person, or number, or it goes.

## Copula dodges (write "is/has" when that is the meaning)

serves as, stands as, functions as, acts as, boasts, features, represents — measured >10% drop of is/are in LLM-edited text; the dodge is the tell.

## -ing significance trails (rewrite the sentence to end on the fact)

", highlighting…", ", underscoring…", ", reflecting…", ", showcasing…", ", emphasizing…"

## Transition-word paragraph openers (move the connective inside, or cut)

Additionally, Moreover, Furthermore, In addition, Notably, Importantly, Interestingly · Vietnamese: tuy nhiên, đồng thời, bên cạnh đó, hơn nữa, ngoài ra

## Vietnamese tells (commonly reported; lower confidence than the English list — no corpus study yet)

- "không chỉ… mà còn…", "không phải X, mà là Y" (negative parallelism)
- "đóng vai trò quan trọng/then chốt", "góp phần" (vague contribution)
- "trong bối cảnh / trong thời đại / trong xu thế [X]" (era-framing openers)
- "Điều đáng chú ý là", "Điều quan trọng là", "cần lưu ý rằng", "hãy nhớ rằng" (throat-clearing)
- "tóm lại / nhìn chung / có thể thấy rằng / có thể nói rằng" (boilerplate endings)
- "một cách hiệu quả / một cách toàn diện" (adverbial padding)
- Hán-Việt padding where a plain word fits: "tối ưu hóa" → "làm tốt hơn", "triển khai" → "làm/chạy"
